Operational Pricing & Negotiation SOP for Staff

Ground policies for quoting rates, hill trip surcharges, and negotiation thresholds.

Scooter Mountain / Outstation Rule (+₹100/day)

For commuter scooters (Activa 125, Jupiter 110, Access 125, Avenis 125, Grazia 125):

  • Local Siliguri Ride: Quoted at base rate (₹600 or ₹700/day).
  • Mountain Ride (Darjeeling, Sikkim, Mirik, Kurseong): Mandatory +₹100/day extra (₹700 or ₹800/day) to cover engine wear & hill maintenance.

Ntorq Regular BS4 Negotiation Rule (₹700/day)

Standard rate for all 7 Regular Ntorq units is ₹800/day.

  • Only for the 2 BS4 units (WB74AX7679 & WB74BB5945):
  • On heavy customer negotiation, staff is authorized to offer ₹700/day, strictly for bookings longer than 2 days (>2 days).

Plate Type Compliance Protocol

  • 5 units carry verified Commercial plates.
  • Remaining 42 units must be addressed as "Under conversion process".
  • NEVER use the word "Private" when speaking or messaging with customers.

WhatsApp Showcase Video Sending Rule

  • All 47 vehicles have dedicated under-30-second HD showcase clips.
  • When customer asks for videos of a model (e.g. "Xpulse ka video bhejo"), staff/bot sends all available videos for that model.
  • Include disclaimer: "Exact bike/color choice is subject to availability at pickup; an available vehicle from this model will be allocated."
